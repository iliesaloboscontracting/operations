# Daily site log: how to (for Damien and Tom)

*One page. For the pilot of the daily log on your supervisor link. Draft, 25 Oct 2026. Hand this over when the log
is switched on for you.*

## What it is and why

At the end of each day, spend **about a minute** telling the office what the crew worked on.
- The office already knows **who was on site and for how long**, from the sign-ins.
- What it doesn't know is **what that time went on**, what held you up, and what went to the tip.
- That's what lets us compare jobs against what was priced, and spot problems early. It also means fewer phone
  calls asking you.

**Nothing on your link ever shows prices or costs.** You only record hours, codes, delays, dockets and progress.

## Every day (about 60 seconds)

Open your usual link (the Lobos Site page you use for sign-ins and requests) and tap **Daily log**.

1. **Crew:** check the list. It's taken from today's sign-ins, with the total hours shown. If it looks wrong (someone
   missing, someone who left early), tap **Something's wrong** and say what. The office will fix the sign-ins.
2. **What did the crew do:** tap the codes you worked on today. Then spread the hours across them with − / +, or tap
   **Split evenly**. The hours left to place must reach 0. For any machine on the board, pick what it mainly did.
   - Rough is fine: whole hours are good enough.
   - Not sure which code? Pick the closest. The codes match the quote, e.g. STR-02 ceilings, STR-03 partitions,
     DEM-10 waste load-out.
3. **Anything hold you up?** Tap **No**, or pick the reason: weather, access, client, plant breakdown, waiting on
   others, or other. Add about how long, a short note and a photo if it helps.
4. **Tip dockets:** tap **None today**, or add each docket. Pick the waste type (e.g. Mixed C&D, Clean heavy, GSW,
   Asbestos), the amount (tonnes, m³, loads or bins), and **take a photo of the docket**. A photo is required for
   asbestos.
5. Add a note or photos of the day if you want, then tap **Submit**. The card turns green: "Logged ✓".

**Made a mistake, or forgot?** Open the log again and fix it - the same day, or before midday the next morning. It
saves a new version, and the office sees the latest.

## Once a week (Friday)

The card also asks for **Progress this week**. For each part of the job, move the slider to roughly how far through
it is (e.g. 60%). It starts at last week's number. If you move one **down**, say why (rework, scope change).

## Reminders

If a job ran and there's no log by **7pm**, you'll get **one** reminder on your phone. Make sure phone alerts are
switched on for your link: the **Reminders** box near the top of its home page (the same switch as the sign-out
reminders).
There's no second nag the same night.

## Good to know

- **Two leads on one job?** Either can log. If both do, the office sees the latest, and who sent it.
- **Covering for someone with a one-day link?** You can log that job from it.
- **No sign-ins today?** The log lets you enter the crew hours by hand. The office will see it was entered that way.
- **Questions or the codes don't fit?** Tell Ilies. The list will be adjusted from what you find in the pilot.
  That's the point of the two weeks.
